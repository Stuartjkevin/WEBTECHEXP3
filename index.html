The dive

Three zones. One dive, every time.

Fathom's timer is built around what actually happens to attention over a session — not arbitrary intervals.

0–30 MIN · SUNLIGHT ZONE

Getting your bearings

The itch to check your phone is strongest here. Fathom holds notifications and shows a gentle countdown so you can push through the first pull toward distraction.

30–90 MIN · TWILIGHT ZONE

Where the real work starts

Most people never get here. The screen dims slightly, sound fades to a low ambient hum, and the interface gets out of your way almost entirely.

90+ MIN · MIDNIGHT ZONE

Full immersion

Reserved for the sessions that matter. Fathom logs these separately — your personal dive log of the deepest work you've done this month.
